NextGen

A hands-on construction trades program introducing young people to real skills, mentors, and career pathways.

Learn a trade. Build confidence. Create a future.

NextGen is designed for high school students and will feature instruction in electrical, carpentry, plumbing, drywall and paint, and HVAC.
