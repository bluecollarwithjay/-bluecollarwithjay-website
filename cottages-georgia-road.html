Job Site Spotlight • Woodlawn, Birmingham

The Cottages on Georgia Road

Building homes. Strengthening Woodlawn. Creating new affordable housing opportunities.

JOB SITE SPOTLIGHTTHE COTTAGESGeorgia Road • Woodlawn
Community-Focused Development

A project rooted in neighborhood investment.

The Cottages on Georgia Road is a housing development led by Woodlawn United and supported by a team committed to expanding affordable housing opportunities in Birmingham’s historic Woodlawn community.

Phase One: 8 cottages
Phase Two: 12 additional units

Project leadership

The people behind the project.

Owner

Woodlawn United

Led by President & CEO Mashonda Taylor, with Joe Ayers, Real Estate Director, helping lead the development effort.

Building Contractor

Helping turn the development vision into a quality-built reality.

Housing & Opportunity

Adding new housing options while supporting continued neighborhood growth and investment.
