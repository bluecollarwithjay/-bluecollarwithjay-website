BLUE COLLAR SPOTLIGHTWILL GOODMANGoodman & Goodman Electric
President & Co-Founder

A career built on curiosity and leadership.

Will Goodman is President and Co-Founder of Goodman & Goodman Electric, established with Gwen Goodman in 2019.

With more than 40 years of electrical experience—including 27 years in electrical construction—Will has built a career defined by technical excellence and the development of others.

The Beginning

Curiosity became a career.

An early interest in how electrical devices work led to formal electrical training in the military as a communications technician.

Greatest Accomplishment

Developing talent.

Will has helped groom successful leaders, journeymen, and master electricians throughout the industry.

Legacy

Build people while building projects.

His story proves that leadership is measured by the people you help grow.
