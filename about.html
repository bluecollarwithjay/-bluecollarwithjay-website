About Jay

Construction professional, storyteller, advocate, and founder of Blue Collar With Jay.

Why this exists

Giving hard work the respect it deserves.

Jay created Blue Collar With Jay to recognize the people whose work often goes unseen. The platform is rooted in construction, but its mission reaches beyond the trades to anyone making a meaningful difference.

Through interviews, jobsite visits, company features, community spotlights, merchandise, and NextGen workforce development, Blue Collar With Jay is building a permanent home for stories worth preserving.
