About the platform

More than media.

Blue Collar With Jay was founded on one belief: the people behind the work deserve to be seen, respected, and remembered.

Meet Jay

The founder

Construction has never just been a career to Jay. It has been a way to serve people, solve problems, and strengthen communities.

Blue Collar With Jay was created to give a professional platform to skilled tradespeople, business owners, educators, veterans, mentors, and community leaders whose contributions too often go unnoticed.

The mission: Honor the Work. Respect the People. Build the Future.
